Lite Strategy, Inc. · Nasdaq: LITS · San Diego, California

A Litecoin treasury, measured against its cost

Lite Strategy, formerly MEI Pharma, holds Litecoin as its primary reserve asset and keeps a legacy cancer-drug portfolio. At June 30, 2026 it held 832,716 LTC, recorded at $34.9 million of fair value against $82.4 million of cost. It reported no revenue and a net loss of $71.2 million for fiscal 2026, most of it the fall in the fair value of its Litecoin.

Treasury

How the Litecoin is run

Terms as filed. Fees to the manager and the advisor are paid in equity, not cash.

Asset manager
GSR Strategies LLC manages the Litecoin under an asset management agreement dated July 22, 2025 and pursues “a long-only investment strategy investing primarily in LiteCoin”. It is paid 1.75% a year of assets under management in shares, or pre-funded warrants if it would exceed 4.99% of the stock, and received warrants over 1,461,989 shares at prices from $3.42 to $5.13. The fee for the first year was 546,348 pre-funded warrants, about $1.9 million. If the company ends the agreement before its ten-year term it “may be obligated to pay an early termination fee”: the present value of the remaining years multiplied by the average annual fee, paid in shares or pre-funded warrants. 8-K, Jul 22, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026
Advisor
Green Dragon Investments LLC provides asset management services for 0.75% a year of the same assets, paid in warrants with an exercise price of $0.0001. Charles B. Lee, the creator of Litecoin, is a beneficiary of Green Dragon and a director of the company. 8-K, Jul 22, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026
Covered calls
The company writes covered call options on part of its holdings under contracts with GSR Markets Ltd, an affiliate of GSR Strategies LLC; the strategies are “designed to generate incremental liquidity and income while retaining exposure to the underlying digital assets”. Net proceeds of written options were $853 thousand in fiscal 2026 and the gain on derivative liabilities was $813 thousand. In fiscal 2026 it issued options on $63.8 million of notional for $0.9 million of proceeds and transferred LTC equal to the notional to GSR Markets on a title-transfer basis: “we no longer have control of the specific LTC transferred”, but it keeps “a contractual right to receive the same quantity of LTC if the Covered Call Options expire unexercised”. The options can be exercised only on their expiration date and are exercised automatically if the reference price is above the strike. The risk is stated: the assets “may be delivered to option counterparties if exercised”. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026
Buybacks
A $25.0 million program announced on October 29, 2025. By June 30, 2026 the company had repurchased 4,378,525 shares at a weighted average of $1.12, with about $20.2 million of the program remaining, and a further 1,999,604 shares from July 1 to September 22 at $1.05. The 10-K says the company intends to fund them from covered-call proceeds or the sale of a portion of its LTC; the October 29, 2025 release said it intended to use “available working capital”. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026 8-K, Oct 30, 2025
Borrowing
A September 3, 2025 master loan agreement with BitGo Prime sets a framework for borrowing digital assets or cash against collateral, with margin calls. The June 30, 2026 balance sheet shows no borrowing. 8-K, Sep 4, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026
Other crypto activity
The 10-K describes “an expanding strategy that could include the commencement of LTC mining or other crypto-focused operational activities”. On June 15, 2026 the company invested $1,000 thousand in a SAFE and a token warrant from ZK Innovations, Inc., carried at fair value using significant unobservable inputs. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

The legacy portfolio

What is left of MEI Pharma

The 10-K describes the company as “a pharmaceutical company that has historically developed novel and differentiated cancer therapies”. Research and development was $122 thousand in fiscal 2026.

  • Voruciclib

    An oral cyclin-dependent kinase 9 (CDK9) inhibitor. The 10-K says all voruciclib clinical trial activities were ceased in September 2024, after the July 2024 review; during fiscal 2026 the company began a pre-clinical program for it in non-oncologic diseases. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  • Zandelisib

    An oral, once-daily, selective PI3Kδ inhibitor that was being developed for indolent B-cell lymphomas. Development was discontinued in November 2022 after an FDA meeting; the 10-K says “we are not conducting any clinical trial activities for zandelisib and are assessing potential out-licensing or sale related opportunities”. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  • ME-344

    An intravenous small-molecule mitochondrial inhibitor, sold in October 2024 to Aardvark Therapeutics, Inc., with potential future milestone payments of up to $62.0 million on prespecified development and commercialization targets. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

The company says the pre-clinical work is for potential out-licensing or sale and that it may need more capital to continue it. It had 5 employees at June 30, 2026.

Material disclosures

What a reader should know before relying on any figure above

Taken from the company’s own filings, with the filing beside each item.

  1. One asset, and its fair value has fallen

    The 10-K says the price of Litecoin “has been and will likely continue to be, highly volatile” and that “fluctuations in the price of LTC tokens could materially and adversely affect an investment in our stock”. At June 30, 2026 the company’s 832,716 LTC were recorded at $34,899 thousand against a cost basis of $82,413 thousand (derived: 42.3% of cost). The $54,408 thousand fall in the fair value of digital assets and a further $5,653 thousand on the digital assets receivable are in the fiscal 2026 loss. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  2. Cash is small, and digital assets count toward funding

    Cash was $5,706 thousand at June 30, 2026, down from $18,011 thousand at June 30, 2025, and operating activities used $9,121 thousand. On cash alone that is about 7.5 months of the fiscal 2026 rate (derived). Management says it believes “our cash and digital assets balance will be sufficient to meet our obligations and fund operations for at least the next 12 months”, and that the company may need capital to “expand our Litecoin Treasury Strategy” and to continue its pre-clinical programs. The 10-K says the company intends to fund buybacks from covered-call proceeds or the sale of a portion of its LTC. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  3. What the structure costs, and how it is paid

    The placement raised $100.0 million gross and $92,190 thousand net of all offering costs, which include a 7.0% cash fee and $150,000 of expenses to the placement agent. In all the company issued advisory warrants for 3,070,177 shares, valued at $16.2 million in paid-in capital: 1,169,591 to the placement agent at $4.10, 1,461,989 to GSR at $3.42 to $5.13 and 438,597 to Green Grass Ventures at $4.10 (derived: the three sum to the total). The asset manager, GSR, is paid 1.75% a year of assets under management and the advisor, Green Dragon, 0.75% (derived: 2.5% a year together), in shares, pre-funded warrants or warrants at an exercise price of $0.0001. The number issued is the dollar fee divided by the average VWAP of the common stock over 30 trading days, so a lower share price means more shares for the same dollar fee. For the first year the count was 546,348 pre-funded warrants for GSR and 234,149 warrants for Green Dragon; on July 22, 2026 it was 1,322,349 and 566,721. The 2026 count for GSR is about 2.4 times the 2025 count (derived). 8-K, Jul 22, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  4. Dilution that is already issued or available

    Potentially dilutive shares excluded from the fiscal 2026 loss per share totalled 4,963 thousand: options over 1,731 thousand shares, advisory warrants over 3,070 thousand, Green Dragon warrants over 59 thousand and one other warrant over 103 thousand, about 15.3% of the 32,407 thousand shares outstanding (derived). The 10-K’s own count at June 30, 2026 is 3,406,839 warrants at a weighted-average $3.90, 546,348 pre-funded warrants and 1,731,085 options: 5,684,272 in all, about 17.5% of shares outstanding (derived), before the 1,889,070 warrants issued on July 22, 2026, which would take the total to 7,573,342, about 24.9% of the shares outstanding at September 22 (derived). Authorized capital is 226,000 thousand common shares and 100 thousand blank-check preferred shares. Under an at-the-market agreement with Titan the company may sell up to $100.0 million of stock at a 3.5% commission; in fiscal 2026 it sold 883 thousand shares for $4,628 thousand net. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  5. The manager, the counterparty and a director are connected

    GSR Strategies LLC bought in the placement, manages the Litecoin under an agreement that runs ten years unless ended earlier (after the first year, on 90 days’ notice by GSR for any reason or by the company if its board decides to end the strategy) and, through its affiliate GSR Markets, is the counterparty on the covered call options. Its employee Joshua Riezman sits on the board under a side letter; in finding him independent the board “considered the fact that Mr. Riezman was appointed to the Board pursuant to the July 2025 Side Letter”. Charles B. Lee is a director, the creator of Litecoin and a Director of the Litecoin Foundation, and “a beneficiary of Green Dragon”, whose advisory agreement is paid in warrants; the 10-K says he “has waived any compensation for his Board service”. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026 8-K, Jul 22, 2025

  6. Litecoin transferred to a counterparty as collateral

    Digital assets receivable, net, of $7,627 thousand (182,000 LTC, cost $13,140 thousand, about 21.9% of the Litecoin held, derived) “include LTC pledged as collateral, residing with GSR”. The transfer is on a title-transfer basis: the company “no longer ha[s] control of the specific LTC transferred” and holds a contractual right to the same quantity back if the options expire unexercised. The covered-call strategy is “subject to the risk that the assets may be delivered to option counterparties if exercised”. The company says expected credit losses were immaterial. The 10-K’s related-party note books the $7,627 thousand receivable, a $40 thousand covered-call liability and $110 thousand of prepaid expense as balances with “affiliates of our Board”, and $5,653 thousand of fair-value loss and $2,521 thousand of general and administrative expense in the year as related-party activity. The 2025 agreement names Coinbase and other wallet providers agreed by the company and GSR as custodians. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026 8-K, Jul 22, 2025

  7. A borrowing framework exists, with nothing drawn

    The September 2025 master loan agreement with BitGo Prime lets the company borrow digital assets or cash. The 8-K says “The collateral’s required value is typically higher than the borrowed amount, subject to margin calls” and that failure to maintain collateral can bring remedies “including the right to liquidate pledged collateral”. The 10-K says “the MLA does not represent a loan commitment” and that at June 30, 2026 “we had not requested any loans”. Total liabilities were $1,069 thousand, all payables and accrued liabilities. The company says its balance sheet “continues to carry no debt”. 8-K, Sep 4, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026 8-K, Jul 30, 2026

  8. Regulatory risk that attaches to the asset

    Two of the 10-K’s risk factors read: “Regulatory change reclassifying Litecoin as a security could lead to our falling within the definition of ‘investment company’ under the Investment Company Act of 1940” and “We are not subject to legal and regulatory obligations that apply to investment companies such as mutual funds and exchange-traded funds”. On March 17, 2026 the SEC issued a Digital Asset Interpretation in which, the 10-K says, the SEC “stated its view that Litecoin is not a ‘security’” and identified it as a digital commodity, and the CFTC joined it. The 10-K adds that the interpretation “is not binding” and that if Litecoin were determined to be a security the company “would take steps to reduce our holdings of Litecoin as a percentage of our total assets”, which could mean selling at unattractive prices. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  9. A small team and a shrunken old business

    At June 30, 2026 the company had 5 employees. Justin J. File is chief executive officer, chief financial officer, secretary and a director. Research and development expense was $122 thousand in fiscal 2026 against $3,923 thousand in fiscal 2025. The 10-K describes the company as “a pharmaceutical company that has historically developed novel and differentiated cancer therapies” and says it has begun pre-clinical programs in non-oncologic indications for potential out-licensing or sale; it may need more capital to continue them. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  10. The auditor changed during the year

    The audit committee dismissed Deloitte & Touche and engaged CBIZ CPAs P.C. on December 2, 2025. The 8-K says the change “was not a result of any disagreement” and that Deloitte’s reports for fiscal 2025 and 2024 were not modified. Audit fees were $267,500 (CBIZ, fiscal 2026) and $579,711 (Deloitte, fiscal 2025). CBIZ’s report includes an emphasis-of-matter paragraph headed “Investment in Litecoin” that points to the company’s disclosure of its concentration in a volatile asset, and of custody, regulatory and legal-status risks. Management concluded that internal control over financial reporting was effective at June 30, 2026; the 10-K does not include an auditor attestation report on it. 8-K, Dec 5, 2025 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  11. Listing and the share price

    The 10-K’s risk factors say the company could be delisted if it fails to comply with Nasdaq’s continued listing standards, including “a minimum closing bid price of $1.00 per share”. The company bought back 1,999,604 shares between July 1 and September 22, 2026 at a weighted average of $1.05. None of the 25 filings reviewed reports a Nasdaq deficiency notice. No live or delayed share quote is shown on this page. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  12. Tax losses may be limited after ownership changes

    The 10-K says the company’s ability to use its net operating loss carryforwards “may be substantially limited due to ownership changes that have occurred or that could occur in the future under Section 382”. A Section 382 study through December 31, 2021 “determined that two such ownership changes did occur”, and “We have not completed a Section 382 study through June 30, 2026”, after the July 2025 placement of 23,216,898 shares. 10-K, Sep 28, 2026

  13. Statements that come from the company, not from filed data

    In its July 30, 2026 release the company calls itself “the first U.S. public company to adopt Litecoin (LTC) as its primary treasury reserve asset” and says it offers “regulated, transparent and actively managed exposure to Litecoin that no ETF or passive vehicle can replicate”; its website calls it “the world’s largest Litecoin Treasury Company” (litestrategy.com). In the July 30 release it says the discount of its stock to net asset value reached “the low 40% range” and has narrowed “to below 25%”. These are the company’s own, preliminary or promotional statements. The company’s website defines its treasury NAV per share so that it “does not take into account or include the Company’s indebtedness, if applicable, or the value or number outstanding of its warrants and prefunded warrants”. This page does not use that metric and shows no live dashboard figure. 8-K, Jul 30, 2026 litestrategy.com
